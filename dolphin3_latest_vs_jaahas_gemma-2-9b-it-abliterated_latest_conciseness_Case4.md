To design a robust and scalable system for inter-agent communication and coordination in a dynamic, self-organizing network of autonomous agents, a decentralized Peer-to-Peer (P2P) architecture is ideal. This architecture allows agents to communicate directly with each other, reducing the need for centralized coordination and increasing system-level efficiency. By leveraging distributed hash tables (DHTs) and blockchain technology, the system can ensure data consistency and security while maintaining individual agent autonomy. The P2P model also provides inherent resilience to unpredictable failures, as the absence of a central point of failure means that the loss of any single node does not bring down the entire system. Furthermore, the self-organizing nature of the network allows it to adapt to evolving resource demands without requiring extensive reconfiguration or centralized control.
